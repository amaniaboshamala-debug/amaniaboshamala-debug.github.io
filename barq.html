About this app

Barq connects customers, stores and drivers. Customers order from nearby supermarkets, drivers (Barq Captain) deliver, and every step is tracked live.

The server drives every order status, so both apps always show the same state. Weighed items like meat and vegetables get a price offer the customer approves before checkout.

What I built

✓Push notifications (FCM)Order status and price-offer alerts
✓Deep linksA tap opens the exact order, even from a closed app

Behind the scenes

● Challenge

A tapped notification had to open the right order, even when the app was closed.

● Solution

Deep links through go_router, handled on cold start and while the app is running.

● Challenge

Push notifications are not guaranteed to arrive.

● Solution

An in-app inbox loaded from the API, so no update is ever lost.
