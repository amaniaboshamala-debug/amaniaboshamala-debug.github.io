About this app

Smilo is a booking app for a dental clinic. Patients browse services and dentists, pick a free time slot and get a QR code to check in at the clinic.

A live queue shows their turn in real time, a smart waitlist offers freed-up slots, and an AI assistant answers questions about services and prices.

What I built

✓Full booking flowTime slots, confirmation, rescheduling and cancellation
✓Live queue & waitlistReal-time turn tracking and freed-up slot offers
✓AI assistantChat about services and prices, through a secure proxy

Behind the scenes

● Challenge

The live queue had to stay up to date without draining battery or data.

● Solution

The app polls the server only while the queue screen is visible, and stops as soon as the user leaves it.

● Challenge

The AI assistant needs an API key, and keys inside an app can be extracted.

● Solution

A Cloudflare Worker sits between the app and the AI, so the key stays on the server, and tokens are kept in Flutter Secure Storage.
